# UniShield - iExec Feedback

## 1. Executive Summary
**Project:** UniShield (Privacy-Preserving Compliance for Uniswap v4)

**Overall Experience:**
Developing with iExec was a super nice experience. The combination of off-chain privacy and on-chain verification was exactly what we needed to build our compliance layer.

---

## 2. What We Loved 
* **Documentation & Evolution:** The documentation is excellent. We were especially impressed to see that our feedback from **ETHrome** was taken into account and actually implemented. It feels like the platform is evolving with its developers.
* **Support:** The team is incredibly responsive and helpful whenever we hit a blocker.
* **DataProtector SDK:** The abstraction provided by the SDK made encrypting user data on the client side seamless and intuitive.

## 3. Friction Points 
* **Testnet Instability:** We occasionally faced downtime with the Workerpool or issues downloading **Task Logs**.
    * *Note:* While this was frustrating during a hackathon crunch, we noticed these issues were resolved very quickly by the team.

## 4. Conclusion
iExec provided the critical "Trustless Privacy" layer that standard oracles couldn't offer. We really appreciate the team's dedication to improving the documentation based on previous hackathons.

**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) - *Great tech, responsive team, and visible improvements since ETHrome.*
